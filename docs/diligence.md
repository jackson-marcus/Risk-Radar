# Vendor Due-Diligence Policy

## financial-review
Every new vendor undergoes a financial health review: two years of statements or a D&B rating. Vendors younger than two years, or with a downgrade or late filing in the last 12 months, require an enhanced review signed off by finance and a shortened 12-month contract term.

## security-soc2
Vendors that touch production systems or customer data must hold a current SOC 2 Type II report (or ISO 27001 certificate) renewed annually. A lapsed report triggers a 60-day remediation window; no new data flows may be added during the lapse.

## pii-dpa
Any vendor processing personal data requires an executed Data Processing Agreement covering purpose limitation, sub-processor disclosure, breach notification within 72 hours, and deletion on termination. PII processing without a DPA is a contract blocker.

## exit-plan
Critical vendors require a documented exit plan: data export format and timeline, transition assistance clauses, and an identified alternative supplier. The exit plan is tested on paper annually.

## concentration-limit
No single-source vendor may exceed 15% of category spend or $400k annually without a risk acceptance signed by the business owner and procurement. Concentration above the limit requires either a second source or an approved exit plan.

## sla-credits
Contracts include measurable SLAs with service credits. Three SLA breaches in a rolling quarter trigger a formal service review; five trigger the termination-for-cause clause review.

## incident-notification
Vendors must notify security incidents affecting our data within 24 hours of discovery. Repeated security events (three or more in a year) require an updated penetration-test summary and a joint remediation plan.

## access-reviews
Vendor personnel access to our systems is reviewed quarterly. Named-account access only; shared credentials are prohibited. Access is revoked within one business day of vendor staff offboarding.

## subprocessors
Vendors disclose all sub-processors and notify changes 30 days in advance. We may object to a sub-processor on reasonable grounds; continued use after objection is a breach.

## insurance
Vendors carry cyber liability insurance of at least $2M per incident and general commercial liability of $1M. Certificates of insurance are collected at onboarding and renewal.

## bcp-testing
Critical vendors provide business-continuity and disaster-recovery summaries, including RTO/RPO targets, and evidence of an annual test. RTO above 24 hours for a critical service requires a compensating control.

## renewal-benchmark
Renewals above $100k are benchmarked against at least two market alternatives. Price increases above 8% year-over-year require a competitive quote or an executive exception.
